# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious visitors encountering a personal hobby project in a browser. They are here to pause, explore, and feel the scale and beauty of Earth rather than complete a conventional task.

## Product Purpose

Create a cinematic, interactive digital monument to Earth. Success means the visitor lingers, moves the globe, and leaves with a renewed sense of wonder.

## Brand Personality

Silent, orbital, crystalline. The experience should feel composed like an Apple product film and spatially convincing like Google Earth, without imitating either product.

## Anti-references

Avoid science-fiction HUD clutter, climate-campaign or activism messaging, corporate marketing polish, generic SaaS structures, and decorative space-tech interfaces.

## Design Principles

- Let Earth be the interface; controls and copy stay subordinate.
- Build awe through scale, light, and pacing rather than dramatic claims.
- Make every movement feel physical, slow, and intentional.
- Keep the story human without becoming promotional or prescriptive.
- Preserve the mystery of space with restraint and silence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special compliance target was requested. Maintain semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, and a reduced-motion experience as baseline craft.
